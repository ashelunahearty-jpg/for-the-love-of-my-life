Something I Want You To Know

I know we're hurting right now. I know there are things we both wish we could have said differently. But I want you to know that I never stopped caring about you.

When I chose to leave, it wasn't because you meant nothing to me. It wasn't because I didn't understand you. I did try to understand you, and I cared about what you were feeling. But some of the words between us hurt me deeply, especially because you were the person I never expected to hurt me that way.

I hope someday you understand that protecting my heart doesn't mean I stopped loving you. Sometimes two people can care about each other and still need space because they're hurting each other.

Please don't remember only the painful ending. Remember the laughter, the little moments, the conversations, the love, and all the times we made each other happy.

Take care of yourself. Eat when you're hungry. Rest when you're tired. Be gentle with yourself. You deserve kindness too.

Whatever happens from here, I genuinely hope you find peace and happiness. What we had was real to me, and I'll always be grateful that it happened.

You don't have to solve your whole life tonight. Just get through this little moment, then the next one. Drink some water, take a slow breath, and please be gentle with yourself. I hope this tiny place can make the world feel a little softer for you.
